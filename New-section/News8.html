Access Bank Lagos City Marathon 2026: Africa’s Biggest Road Race Returns

February 2026 · Lagos, Nigeria

The Access Bank Lagos City Marathon 2026 is set to once again transform Nigeria’s commercial capital into a celebration of endurance, speed, and unity. Thousands of elite and amateur runners from across Africa and beyond will take to the streets of Lagos in what has become one of the continent’s most prestigious road races.

From the early morning start, runners will surge through the heart of the city, passing iconic landmarks and drawing massive roadside support from enthusiastic spectators. The marathon continues to grow in global stature, attracting world-class athletes aiming to break records and claim top honors.

Elite Showdown Expected

With international contenders lining up against Nigeria’s finest long-distance runners, the 2026 edition promises a thrilling battle for the podium. Fast course conditions and strong competition could see new course records set as athletes push their limits over the 42.195km distance.

Beyond the elite race, the marathon remains a powerful symbol of community and national pride. Corporate teams, fitness enthusiasts, and first-time marathoners will participate alongside professionals, highlighting the event’s inclusive spirit.

Zpoint Sports Media will be on ground providing full coverage of the Access Bank Lagos City Marathon 2026. From pre-race build-up and athlete features to live race updates, finish-line reactions, and post-race analysis, our team will bring fans closer to every stride.

Expect exclusive interviews, race-day highlights, and behind-the-scenes moments capturing the energy that defines marathon day in Lagos.
