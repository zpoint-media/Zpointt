AFCON 2025 Final: Senegal Break Moroccan Hearts to Retain African Crown

21 Dec 2025

Senegal were crowned champions of Africa once again after a dramatic 2025 AFCON final victory over host nation Morocco. In a night filled with tension, noise, and raw emotion, the defending champions edged past the Atlas Lions to silence a packed Moroccan crowd.

Morocco entered the final dreaming of lifting their first AFCON trophy since 1976 on home soil, but Senegal’s experience, discipline, and composure under pressure proved decisive on the biggest stage.

The final exploded into life early as Morocco dominated possession, pushing forward with confidence and feeding off the energy of their supporters. Their pressure paid off in the 34th minute when Hakim Ziyech fired home a stunning strike from the edge of the box to give the hosts the lead.

Senegal, however, responded like true champions. Just before halftime, Sadio Mané leveled the score with a calm finish following a swift counterattack, sending the teams into the break locked at 1–1.

The second half was a physical and tactical battle, with chances at both ends but no breakthrough. Extra time failed to separate the sides, setting the stage for a nerve-shredding penalty shootout.

In the shootout, Senegal held their nerve. Kalidou Koulibaly converted the decisive penalty as Senegal triumphed 4–2 on penalties, sparking celebrations among players and fans alike.

Man of the Match: Sadio Mané — whose leadership, equalizing goal, and composure embodied Senegal’s championship mentality.

For Morocco, the defeat was heartbreaking, but their journey to the final showcased their growth as a footballing nation. Senegal, meanwhile, etched their name into history by successfully defending their AFCON title and confirming their status as Africa’s dominant force.
