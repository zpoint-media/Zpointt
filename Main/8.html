ISU Short Track World Championships 2026 Set for High-Speed Drama in Montréal

13 – 15 March 2026 · Montréal, Canada

The world’s fastest skaters are heading to Montréal, Canada, as the ISU CNSG Short Track World Championships 2026 takes center stage from March 13–15. With global titles on the line, the championship promises three days of explosive speed, tactical brilliance, and edge-of-the-seat racing.

Hosted in one of North America’s most passionate winter sports cities, this prestigious event will bring together elite athletes from across the globe competing for world supremacy on ice.

The championship will feature the 500m, 1000m, and 1500m individual races for both men and women, alongside the electrifying relay competitions. Short track speed skating is renowned for its intensity — tight turns, high-speed overtakes, and dramatic photo finishes often decide medals by fractions of a second.

Defending champions will be tested by rising stars eager to announce themselves on the world stage. Every lap, every corner, and every exchange in the relays could define the outcome.

The relay events, in particular, are expected to deliver thrilling moments as teams rely on precision, strategy, and flawless timing under immense pressure.

Zpoint Sports Media will be on-site in Montréal providing comprehensive coverage throughout the championship. From race breakdowns and athlete features to medal updates and exclusive reactions, our team will deliver in-depth reporting from start to finish.

Fans can expect daily highlights, expert analysis, and behind-the-scenes content as the battle for world titles unfolds on Canadian ice.

As the season reaches its climax, the ISU CNSG Short Track World Championships 2026 promises unforgettable moments — blistering speed, dramatic finishes, and new champions crowned before a global audience.
